# Privacy & naming policy (package contents)

This kit is meant to be **emailed, zipped, or published** without carrying the presenter’s personal or institutional identity.

## Must not appear anywhere in the distributable package

- Real person names, emails, phone numbers
- Real student / patient / staff identifiers
- Real school, hospital, company, or government unit names
- Real internal spreadsheet IDs or production URLs of a specific org
- Service account private keys

## Allowed

- Fictional scenarios and role titles (`program lead`, `admin`)
- Placeholder emails like `you@example.com`
- Generic product names (Google Sheets, gcloud)
- User-generated `.env` **on their machine only** (gitignored)

## If you fork for a local workshop

Keep **presenter-only notes** outside this folder (or in a private repo).  
Do not merge real class rosters into `docs/` or `scripts/`.
